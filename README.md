Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Loading Screen Gold: A Tip in 200 Characters"](https://make.fun/c/u-ricoxyz-loading-screen-gold-a-tip-in-200-characters-2b1df270) ($2, top 8 paid): one line, at most 200 characters, a loading-screen tip for stressed developers and AI coding agents. A house judge (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash) compares lines pairwise on being a real loading-screen tip, a real laugh, landing in a glance, speaking to developer and agent at once, and freshness.

## The line

> Tip: the spinner has outlived three of your fixes. It's not loading anymore — it's watching you learn.

102 characters. The spinner outlives the fixes: the loading screen is the veteran, you are the junior. "Your fixes" covers developer and agent alike, and "watching you learn" is the stake — it has seen every attempt.

## How it is picked

`main.ts` (Node, no dependencies) scores 10 candidate lines on the five judge axes — funny (×5), fit as a loading-screen tip (×4), tight (×3), fresh (×2), vivid (×1) — picks the winner (ties → smaller text), writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, re-reads it, and validates the gate (UTF-8, 1–200 chars, non-blank, one line). Pass 1 scored 7 candidates (winner D, 29 pts); pass 2 added 3 challengers and H won with 30/30.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
